# Example Prompts

## Campaign continuation

> Continue the existing single-player campaign with a full new chapter. Deep-research verified franchise history, public developer commentary, cut/abandoned concepts, lore, current modding tools, and engine capabilities. Build missions one at a time, launch and playtest them, use logs/OpenCV/crash data/input tests, regression-test previous missions, and keep fixing until the entire campaign is playable.

## Game fusion

> Recreate Minecraft-style first-person movement, block placement/removal, hotbar, inventory, crafting, persistence, and building inside BeamNG.drive while preserving BeamNG's world and vehicle physics. Research both systems deeply, prototype the player and one block first, then expand while monitoring performance and regressions.

## Decomp / ROM transformation

> Turn an N64 action-adventure game into a first-person shooter. Use my uploaded rifle model, adapt it to the original game's poly count, texture style, and hardware limits, place it in the starting room, make it collectible and usable, implement aiming/firing/ammo/damage/UI/save behavior, compile a ROM, launch it in an emulator, and keep fixing until the opening gameplay path works and regression checks pass.
