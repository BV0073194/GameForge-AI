# Project Goal

Create a full professional-quality fan-made single-player campaign continuation to Titanfall 2 titled "Titanfall: Protocol 4". Continue Jack Cooper's story after Titanfall 2, with BT's post-credits signal as the starting point. Deep-research all relevant publicly available and authorized information: Titanfall/Titanfall 2 canon, Respawn developer interviews and public commentary, publicly reported canceled Titanfall 3 work, Titanfall Legends reporting, public game-design talks, public engine/modding research, Northstar source/docs, public campaign scripts/tools, public reverse-engineering research, public archived documentation, and any other useful verified public source. Keep confirmed canon, developer statements, reported information, abandoned/cut concepts, community theories, and our original ideas clearly separated.

Build an actual playable single-player campaign continuation rather than just writing a story. Target approximately 10 substantial missions, each with a distinct gameplay identity, while preserving Titanfall 2's movement, combat feel, military sci-fi tone, Pilot/Titan relationship, pacing, and existing functionality. The campaign should focus on Cooper and BT, with BT returning gradually rather than cheaply or instantly. The opening should begin with Cooper investigating the post-credits helmet signal. Use a verified technically feasible implementation path based on the user's legally installed Titanfall 2 and public/authorized tooling.

GameForge must establish a clean known-good baseline, verify what single-player scripting/map/AI/checkpoint/cinematic/dialogue systems are truly available, build a small vertical slice first, and then expand mission by mission. It must use logs, crash data, game-window/OpenCV analysis, screenshots, process state, automated/scripted input where practical, performance data, and regression tests. Every completed mission becomes part of the regression suite. If later changes break earlier missions, fix or roll back. Continue researching, implementing, building, launching, observing, debugging, repairing, and retesting until the full requested campaign is playable and packaged, or until a genuine documented external/technical blocker prevents further progress.
