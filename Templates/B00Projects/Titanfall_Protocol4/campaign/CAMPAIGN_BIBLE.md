# Protocol 4 Campaign Bible

## Core premise
After Typhon, Cooper's helmet receives an anomalous signal. Investigation suggests fragments associated with BT survived. BT's return is gradual, incomplete, and earned through gameplay/story.

## Tone
- military science fiction first;
- Cooper/BT relationship at the emotional center;
- restrained humor;
- high mobility;
- spectacle used purposefully;
- no forced Apex cameo parade.

## Mission identities
1. Ghost in the Machine — signal investigation / traversal / first proof.
2. Residual — Fold-space residue / environment overlap prototype.
3. Recovery — Pilot-heavy IMC facility infiltration / damaged Vanguard chassis.
4. Protocol Zero — incomplete BT restoration.
5. The Apex Predator — Blisk re-enters with pragmatic motives.
6. Dead Frontier — large combined-arms Pilot/Titan mission.
7. Echoes of Typhon — full environment-overlap mechanic.
8. Legends — carefully connect later Frontier developments.
9. The Last Vanguard — prototype enemy Titan / modular BT upgrades.
10. Protocol 4 — parallel Cooper/BT finale; Cooper must protect BT.

All technical implementation must be adapted to what the engine/toolchain can genuinely support.
